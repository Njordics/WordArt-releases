# WordArt Privacy Policy

*Last updated: 24 October 2026*

WordArt is a single-player coloring and word-search game made by Njordic. It does not sell personal data.

There are two versions. The **GitHub version** has no accounts, no ads and no analytics. The **Google Play
version** adds optional Google Play Games sign-in (for cloud save) and optional rewarded video ads; those two
features are described below and are the only places where data leaves your phone through Google.

## What stays on your phone

Your progress (coins, paint, owned books, colored pages, achievements, settings) is stored only on your device.
If Android backup is turned on in your phone's settings, Android may include this progress in your Google
backup so it can be restored on a new phone; that backup is handled by Google under their privacy policy, and
WordArt cannot read it.

## Google Play Games and cloud save (Google Play version)

If you sign in with Google Play Games, WordArt shows your Play Games name in Settings and saves a copy of your
progress (the same data listed above, without your device settings) to your Play Games account using Google's
Saved Games service, so you can continue on another phone. This is stored by Google under the
[Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy); WordArt has no server of its own and cannot see
anyone else's data. Signing in is optional. You can delete the saved game from the Google Play Games app or
your Google account settings.

## Ads (Google Play version)

The Google Play version contains **rewarded video ads only**: an ad plays only when you tap a button such as
"Watch an ad for +5 spare paint". There are no pop-up or banner ads. Ads are provided by Google AdMob, which may
collect your device's advertising ID, approximate location (from your IP address), and information about ad
views and interactions, to show and measure ads and prevent fraud. See
[How Google uses information from apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).
In the EU, UK and other regions where it is required, WordArt asks for your consent first (Google's consent
form), and you can change your choice at any time in **Settings → Ad privacy options**. You can also reset or
delete your advertising ID in your phone's Google settings.

## Network requests

WordArt connects to the internet only to:

- **Check for new books and download them** from GitHub (`github.com/Njordics/WordArt-releases`).
- **Check for and download app updates** from the same place (versions installed from GitHub only; the Google
  Play version is updated by the Play Store).

These requests send no personal information. (The Google Play version also talks to Google Play services for
sign-in, cloud save and ads, as described above.) Like any website, GitHub receives your IP address and basic
technical data when files are downloaded; see the
[GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

## Crash reports

If WordArt crashes, it saves a report on your phone containing the app version, your Android version and phone
model, and technical details of the error. **Nothing is sent automatically.** On the next launch you can choose
to send it, either as a public GitHub issue (which you submit yourself from your GitHub account) or through any
app you pick, or to discard it.

## Permissions

- **Notifications** (optional): to tell you when an update is available.
- **Set wallpaper**: only when you tap "Set as wallpaper" on one of your pictures.
- **Install packages** (GitHub version only): to install updates you choose to download.
- Saving a picture or video to your gallery happens only when you tap "Save".

## Children

WordArt is suitable for all ages, but the Google Play version is not directed at children under 13. WordArt itself
collects no personal information from anyone; the Google services above are optional (sign-in) or only used
when you choose to watch an ad.
